# v0.4 run on the development corpus: selected findings

These are questions for a reviewer, produced by rule checks. They are not
conclusions about data quality.

## DRC INSP weekly epidemiological profile, SE41 and SE42 2026

Week-41 values were revised in the week-42 report for 20 disease measures,
for example haemorrhagic fever syndrome cases 757 to 693, suspected malaria
506,772 to 500,985, confirmed malaria 319,960 to 316,019, mpox 594 to 587,
measles 386 to 385.

In 14 measures, the year-to-date total rose by less than the new cases
reported for week 42. In every case the difference equals the downward
revision of week 41 (for example mpox: total rose by 532, new cases 539,
week 41 revised by -7). The totals therefore appear to absorb the revision.

Related figures under different definitions in the same report:
- cholera week 41: summary table 1,686 cases; cholera slide 2,330 suspected
  cases (restated as 2,337 in the SE42 report);
- cholera week 40: table 2,729; slide 2,709;
- measles week 41: table 386; measles slide 585 cases;
- cholera week 42 (SE42 report): table 2,513; slide text 1,962 and 2,003.

## INSP daily BVD sitreps No 165 to 171

Recurring challenges linked across reports include: the Butuhe point of
control reported closed since the 26 September security incident; reporting
completeness in Sud-Kivu at 56 percent in all seven reports; treatment
centres in Nord-Kivu close to saturation; blood stock-out in Ituri; health
promoters not trained and lack of transport in Wanie-Rukula (Tshopo).

## WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record No 33 and 41

Selected new signals by region are captured as signal statements. WER 41
states 9,066 confirmed BVD cases and 4,381 confirmed deaths as of 11 October
2026; no INSP report in this corpus covers that date, so no cross-source
comparison was possible.
