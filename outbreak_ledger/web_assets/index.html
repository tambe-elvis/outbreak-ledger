Outbreak Ledger local pilot

Outbreak Ledger maintains an inspectable evidence record across successive reports. Its outputs follow enforced epidemiological rules and named reviewer decisions, so the same evidence and decisions always give the same result, whatever the upload order. One workspace holds reports of several events: each upload is recognised by its report family (currently DRC INSP daily Ebola sitreps, the DRC INSP weekly multi-disease profile and the WHO Weekly Epidemiological Record) and routed automatically. Numbers become reviewable observations; narrative text becomes reviewable qualitative statements linked across reports as issue threads.

Your workflow

  1. Upload digital PDFs; unrecognised layouts are listed, never counted.
  2. Select an observation. Read the source page and extraction location.
  3. Check the date, area, case definition and number.
  4. Record your name, decision and source-review reason.
  5. Export the unambiguous approved observations.

What is automatic?

Extraction and rule checks run after upload. Checks rerun after review. Approval is always an explicit reviewer action. The synthetic example imports test PDFs but does not approve them.

Reconciliation policy OL-RP-1

Cumulative snapshots are never added; their differences are net reported changes. New counts belong only to their stated period. Missing periods are never filled with zeros; a gap is called missing reports only when the report numbering is known to be consecutive. A later value from the same issuer for the same period supersedes an earlier one only when the report states the revision or a reviewer confirms it; otherwise both stay visible and the period is unresolved. Different issuers are shown side by side. The latest reported claim and the latest reviewed value are shown separately.

Current limits

No OCR, AI model, scheduled retrieval, HZ-OPI, institutional authentication or full historical indicator replay is included. Qualitative tags (type, topic, state, place) come from word lists and section headings: they are suggestions with known errors, shown for review. PDF layouts outside the configured modules may fail or require mapping changes. Source support and extraction accuracy require human verification. The review log is not tamper-proof. This is a pilot for testing, not a validated operational system.

Local operation and persistence

Records and source PDFs are stored under local_data beside the application. Reloading the page preserves them. Keep the launcher window open while working; close it to stop the service. Only localhost is exposed in the normal Windows launcher. Internet is required for initial setup, not routine processing. Back up the entire local_data folder after stopping the server.

Public reports can still contain sensitive information

Use aggregate public reports for this pilot. Check names, small counts, locations and narrative context before sharing exports. No files or review content are sent to an external service by the application.
